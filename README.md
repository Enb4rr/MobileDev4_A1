# MobileDev4_A1

### Made by PG29 Julian R
### Last Updated 6/3/2026

A physics-based ball-tilting maze game for Android.

---

## Flow

1. Launch the app and press **Start** on the main menu
2. The game randomly selects one exit and highlights it with a **glowing effect**
3. Tilt the device to roll the ball through the maze towards the glowing exit
4. Reach the glowing exit to complete the level, score is based on how fast you get there
5. Press **Restart** on the result screen to play again with a new random exit

## Native Features: Pinch & Twist Gestures

This project integrates **two-finger touch gestures** as its native hardware feature, extending the core tilt mechanic with camera control.

### Pinch to Zoom
Place two fingers on the screen and move them together or apart to adjust the camera's field of view.

### Twist to Rotate
Keep two fingers on the screen and rotate them to orbit the camera around the ball.
